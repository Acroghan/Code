Live data settings

Kalshi's API refuses requests sent straight from a web page, so live refreshes go through a relay you choose. Until one is set, the page uses the bundled board snapshot. Use {url} where the relay expects the full Kalshi URL.

Presets:

Market numbers come from Kalshi's player ladders: each strike's yes price (mid of bid and ask) is read as P(stat ≥ strike), and strikes quoted wider than 30¢ are ignored. Yardage ladders get a Weibull or lognormal fit; count ladders are read directly. The market fantasy projection scores the implied means with standard rules (0.1/yd, 6 per TD, 0.04/pass yd, 4 per pass TD, −2 per INT, plus the reception value you pick); stats with no market count as zero. The points range and the touchdown scenarios come from sampling each stat independently, so the true spread is somewhat wider and the touchdown games sit a little higher than shown, since big-yardage games and touchdowns go together. Benchmarks are Sleeper (Rotowire) and ESPN weekly projections scored the same way.
